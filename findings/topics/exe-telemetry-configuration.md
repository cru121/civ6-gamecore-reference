# Telemetry configuration compiled into the engine exe

Method: two XML documents are embedded in the exe as string literals (found by root element `<Rulesets>` and `<TelemetryItems>`), parsed by `docs_proto/tools/extract_exe_telemetry.py`. Reference page: `docs_proto/docs/exe/telemetry.md`.
**Verified**: names, flags and counters are literals. **Not determined**: the meaning of `SendFlags` 4 and 8 and the per-event data fields. This is the built-in default; the exe can also fetch `OnlineTelemetryConfiguration.xml` from the Firaxis Live service (discovery step, retries), which may replace it. No opt-out was looked for; nothing here says what a given machine sends.
- Default ruleset (`IsBlacklist`): 54 events, 39 enabled. Examples: GameStart, GameStartPlayers, SaveGameProgression, PlayerProgression (every 10 turns), AIProgression (30), Tutorial, GameEnd, ScreenBreakdown, LensBreakdown, EnvBreakdown, PrjFrequency, PowerStats, WorldCongress, GameLevel (all every 10 turns),
  ApplicationSessionStatus, Transactions, CPU, SysInfo, Timings, GraphicsOptions, SystemConfiguration, LibraryLoad, Hitch, BenchmarkResult, PlayerScore, ReligionFounded. Disabled by default: SlowTick, TickTimings, CameraZoom, GovernmentChanged, BuildingComplete, UnitPromoted, TechBoost, CivicBoost, Game_Boost, PlayerEconomySnapshot, ...
  Derived rulesets `Elite Player Ruleset` and `New Player Ruleset` change the intervals of PlayerProgression, AIProgression and PlayerEconomySnapshot.
- 79 counters in seven tables: UI screens opened (Civilopedia, tech and civic trees, production panel, world rankings, great works ...) and seconds open, lens usage (13 lenses), terrain-state and climate statistics (floods, storms, droughts, eruptions, mitigations), build counts of tunnels, canals and flood barriers, power statistics, World Congress and game level values.
- Behaviour from the messages: queues with priority, sampling and priority rejection, periodic flush and flush on shutdown to `/telemetry/v2`, retries for configuration and flush; log line `Sending Telemetry: OrgEvent=... SendFlags=...` in `GameCore::App::Telemetry::Listener::OnTelemetryTag`; option `[Misc] TelemetryUploadNecessary`.
