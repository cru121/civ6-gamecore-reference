# Game::Culture::SetGreatWorkPlayer

`void __thiscall SetGreatWorkPlayer(Culture* this, int index, PlayerTypes player)`

Writes a player id into the game-wide great work record.

## Verified from the decompilation
- Calls edit() on the tracked list at Culture+0x108, then writes the player at record[index*0x18 + 4].
- The player field is initialised to -1 when the record is created ([FindOrAddGreatWork](Culture_FindOrAddGreatWork.md)), so this call is what fills it in.
- Not called in the trade path (Enact, PostEnact, City::Manager::Transfer). Called from GrantRandomProduct, CreateHeroEpicGreatWork and Player::Operations::Manager::Process.

## Observed in-game (real save, 2026-10-01)
- On a real save from a forum report, the record "player" field of works held by other civilizations was the creator, not the holder; the field is also used for theming, so overwriting it with the holder is not safe.

## Inferred (not confirmed in-game)
- That field is probably the creator of the work, not its current holder; Game.GetGreatWorkPlayer most likely returns the creator and does not change on trade. (Medium; inferred from callers only.)

## For modders
- Do not read this as 'who owns it now'. Use IsInCity across the player's cities for the current holder.
