AttachAreas

Writes - appends unique player pointers to the vector at this+0x98..0xa8

Side effects - may grow the fixed vector (heap allocation on overflow)

Input checks: Skips areas that are not found or whose flag byte is zero; duplicates skipped.

Preconditions - called during portal setup - game thread

Multiplayer: safe-deterministic. Risk: low (internal protected helper building a cached list). Confidence of the reading: low.
