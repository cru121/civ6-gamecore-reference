Notes (★)

SetPlotOwner

Sets or clears the owner of a plot through the WorldBuilder city manager.

  • The recovered signature is empty or partial. Civ6-GCO calls two forms: SetPlotOwner(x, y, false) to clear ownership and SetPlotOwner(x, y, ownerID, cityID) to assign it, clearing first and then assigning. See Plot.SetOwner for the same two-step flip. [inferred; evidence: community-reference — Civ6-GCO Scripts/GCO_PlotScript.lua:404-430; third-party mod Civ6-GCO (Gedemon, 2021), not a Firaxis script]

See also: Plot.SetOwner
