Signatures are recovered from the game code. ⚠ = partly understood (arguments may be missing or approximate). ? after a name = optional argument. Types: integer/number are Lua numbers, enum-typed arguments show the C++ type in brackets. Two signatures mean the method is registered separately for the UI and the gameplay state and each wrapper was decoded on its own. "same code as" in the Symbol column means the compiler merged identical functions: those methods share one wrapper and cannot be told apart by address.

Notes (★)

SetResearchProgress

Sets the research progress of a technology; returns whether it was applied.

  • Civ6-GCO unlocks a hidden gate technology by setting its progress to its full cost, SetResearchProgress(techID, GetResearchCost(techID)), and re-applies the current research progress afterwards. It reports that its ResearchChanged guard did not reliably stop the AI from re-picking locked technologies in the same turn (tested January 2021). [inferred; evidence: community-reference — Civ6-GCO Scripts/GCO_ResearchScript.lua:832-880, 1709; third-party mod Civ6-GCO (Gedemon, 2021), not a Firaxis script]
