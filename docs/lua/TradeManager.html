Notes (★)

GetTradeRoutePath

Returns the plots of a trade route between two cities.

  • TradeManager.GetTradeRoutePath is registered on the UI cache state only. Civ6-GCO reaches it through a UI-context wrapper (GCO.GetTradeManager) even though most of its logic runs in gameplay scripts, which fits. [verified; evidence: dll-registration — TradeManager.GetTradeRoutePath is in the ui-cache table only, community-reference — Civ6-GCO Scripts/GCO_CityScript.lua:1835, 2277; InGame/GCO_ContextFunctions.lua ~178; third-party mod Civ6-GCO (Gedemon, 2021), not a Firaxis script]
  • In GCO's calls the arguments are (originOwner, originCityID, destinationOwner, destinationCityID) and the result is a list of plots. [inferred; evidence: community-reference — Civ6-GCO Scripts/GCO_CityScript.lua:2303; third-party mod Civ6-GCO (Gedemon, 2021), not a Firaxis script]
